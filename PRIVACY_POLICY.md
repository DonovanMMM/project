# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-18
App: Book of Mormon Mobile (`org.donov.mobile_app`)
Developer: Donov
Contact: donov@example.com

## Overview

Book of Mormon Mobile is designed to process app content locally on your device.

## Data Collection

This app does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect personal information such as:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Precise location
- Contacts

## App Data Usage

The app processes scripture text and search/chart inputs provided by the user for in-app functionality.

## Network Usage

The app may request chart image rendering from a remote chart service.
- If remote rendering is unavailable, the app falls back to local rendering behavior.
- No user account or direct personal identifiers are intentionally transmitted as part of normal chart requests.

## Data Sharing

The app does not intentionally sell personal data.

## Security

Reasonable technical steps are used to keep app functionality stable and secure.

## Children

This app is not specifically directed to children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated over time. The "Last updated" date above will be changed when updates are made.
